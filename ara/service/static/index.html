How verification works
No evidence → no claim. The answerer must cite admissible evidence for every factual claim; the verifier re-checks each claim deterministically (numbers must appear in cited pages, match a verified calculator result, or be a bounded derivation). Unsupported answers are refused with a reason — that is the product working, not an error. HIGH-risk tool calls pause and wait for your approval on the Tasks tab.
